# Privacy Policy - Dental Implant Case Management

## Information Collection

### What We Collect

**We Do NOT Collect:**
- We do not collect your personal information
- We do not collect patient data
- We do not send data to external servers
- We do not use analytics or tracking

**What You Store Locally:**
- Patient medical and dental records
- Clinic information
- Treatment plans and clinical notes
- Documents and imaging
- Your account credentials

### Data Storage

**Local Storage Only**
- All data is stored locally on your device
- Database file: `app_data_dir/database/app.sqlite`
- Documents: `app_data_dir/files/patients/`
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- No cloud synchronization unless you explicitly configure it

### Data Ownership

**You Own Your Data**
- All patient data belongs to you
- Data is stored on your device under your control
- You can export your data at any time
- You can create backups for data portability

## Data Security

### Encryption

**Current Security Measures:**
- Passwords are hashed using Argon2 (industry standard)
- Local database files are not encrypted by default
- Backup files are not encrypted (future enhancement planned)

**Recommended Practices:**
- Enable full-disk encryption on your device
- Use strong passwords and security questions
- Keep your operating system updated
- Store backups securely

### Access Control

**Single-User Application**
- Designed for single-user access per installation
- Authentication required for access
- No multi-user roles or permissions
- Audit logging tracks all data access

## Data Sharing

### We Do Not Share Your Data

**No Data Sharing:**
- We do not sell your data
- We do not share patient information
- We do not provide data to third parties
- No data is used for advertising or marketing

### Your Data Sharing

**You Control Sharing:**
- Patient data is never shared unless you choose to
- You can export data for transfer to other systems
- You can generate letters and reports for sharing
- Document sharing is entirely under your control

## Data Retention

### Your Data, Your Rules

**No Automatic Deletion:**
- We do not automatically delete your data
- Patient data remains until you delete it
- No retention limits imposed by us

**Recommended Practices:**
- Follow local healthcare data retention regulations
- Archive old patient records appropriately
- Maintain backups as per regulatory requirements
- Securely dispose of data when no longer needed

## Security Breaches

### No Cloud Data Breach Risk

**Local-Only Architecture:**
- Since no data is stored in the cloud, there's no cloud breach risk
- Security depends on your device security
- Physical device security is important
- Regular backups protect against device loss

### Your Responsibility

**Device Security:**
- Keep your device secure
- Use device encryption (BitLocker, FileVault)
- Maintain strong passwords
- Don't leave device unattended in insecure locations
